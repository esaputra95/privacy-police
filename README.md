# privacy-police

Static Privacy Policy pages for Google Play applications, configured for deployment on Vercel (`cleanUrls: true`).

## Routes
- `/` — App directory
- `/office` — Office App Privacy Policy
- `/crm` — CRM App Privacy Policy
- `/daily-sunnah` — Daily Sunnah Privacy Policy (`id.kasirq.dailysunnah`)
- `/mahjong-zen` — Mahjong Zen Privacy Policy (`id.binarydev.mahjongzen`)
